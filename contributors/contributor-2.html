THE CONTRIBUTORS

Prince Matthew C. Bugarin

Role: Writer • Researcher

One of his goals is to become an engineer in the future. His interest in civil engineering comes from seeing how construction and engineering can turn ideas into actual buildings and structures. He hopes to learn more about designing and building structures that are safe, useful, and beneficial to people. He also wants to make his family proud and contribute something meaningful to his community in the future. Aside from going to school, Matthew enjoys playing video games, watching anime, and doing things that he finds interesting. This allows him to unwind and have a breather from the pressures of school. Matthew also loves being around his loved ones, whom he considers as motivators.
