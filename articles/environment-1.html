The Environment as a Literary Theme

The article explains how literature has explored the relationship between humans and nature. Writers have used the environment to show how nature affects people, how people affect nature, and how society changes its relationship with the natural world.

Main idea: The environment in literature isn’t simply a background or setting. It can influence characters, shape their identities, reflect society’s problems, and represent humanity’s relationship with the natural world.
