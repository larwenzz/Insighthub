Designing structures for the benefit of people

One of the needs that humans need to live is shelter. Shelter has been a protection for any people that live within an environment, whether there will be natural disasters, or from anything that will bring them harm. These are the basic necessities for people, however there are still some that live under the streets, or even bridges that people pass through. How come not all of us have shelters that meet the benefits of how it can actually protect us?

People settle for structures that are built with wood, together with roofing sheets and top it off with a rubber tire to help the roof to be stable when it comes to strong winds and rains. All of these already serve as a shelter, but worse is living inside a cardboard box. The material may already serve as a matting, a blanket, and even a roof, but not for long.

People are not blind to witness these kinds of variants. They will probably walk away, or even give them even the little necessities such as coins, or even small pieces of food that keeps them alive. Everything that they see around them serves as a shelter for them, even open space that has a platform for them to sit and sleep on.

However, these structures are not guaranteed to last long enough for the span of lifetime. They have to be innovated to match the change of calamities, the change of foundation and the structure that has been planned by the architects. They can have the ability to stand strong under certain events, but some remain unfinished or worse, for some projects that have remained untouched.

Our environment also has a capacity on how many structures are there to be built. Too much may cause a lack of natural resources and less space for trees to be planted. It may bring some benefits, but small changes are preferable enough for innovation.

A small park around would help people to stay in those areas for a period of time, and can also be a better shelter for the people who need it. We must sustain balance in each step of opportunities the spaces which can be filled.

Still, it does not mean that there will be a structure to be built, they will build it right away. We must first consider what are the parts of it that will be affected, of how will be the wellbeing of the people who surround it.

Building structures may be dangerous as it may use a lot of pollution. Some projects are built because it is convenient for the people, and not focusing on what can actually benefit. Some projects are just a hassle to some, a waste of resources, in addition to exchange, the workers receive low loads of payment.
